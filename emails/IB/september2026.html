Three ways to help your student

1. Check Canvas together

Open Grades in Canvas and look for missing work, late submissions, and teacher feedback. A short weekly check is usually enough.

2. Encourage revision and communication

Students should use feedback to improve their work and contact me when they need help, need to submit late work, or want to discuss a revision.

3. Ask about the physics

Ask your student to explain an experiment, graph, model, or physics idea from class. Explaining their thinking is one of the best ways to strengthen understanding.
